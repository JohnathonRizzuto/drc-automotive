What we do

Full-service repair & tires

One shop for everything your car needs — from a quick tire patch to engine diagnostics. If it drives, we fix it.

Tires

New and quality used tires, mounting, rotation, balancing, and 24-hour tire repair to get you back on the road fast.

Brakes & Suspension

Pads, rotors, shocks, and struts — the parts that keep you stopping straight and riding smooth.

A/C & Heating

Recharge, leak repair, and climate fixes so you're comfortable through Illinois summers and winters alike.

Tune-Ups & Maintenance

Oil changes, filters, plugs, fluids, and factory-schedule upkeep that keeps small problems from becoming big ones.

Exhaust & Muffler

Diagnostics & Repair

Check-engine lights, no-starts, electrical gremlins — we diagnose it right and fix it for any make or model.

Who we are

A family shop that earns it

DRC Automotive has been Bristol's go-to garage since 2011 — family-owned, family-run, and built on a simple idea: treat people the way you'd want your own family treated.

That means we tell you what your car actually needs, not what pads the bill. We show you the worn part, give you a straight estimate, and stand behind the work. It's why so many of our customers have been coming back for years — and why they send their friends.

Whatever you drive, you'll get the same thing every time: honest answers, fair pricing, and a car that's ready when we say it will be.
